About

Quiet Margin Studio 餘白齋 is the imprint of WK Ong, a writer and student of calligraphy based in Singapore. The name means "the studio of white space": the quiet margin where a page is allowed to breathe, and where a reader can pause.

The books are made for readers who want to understand classical Chinese texts, not only admire them: poetry with careful commentary, learner editions that make a long story readable, and calligraphy references drawn from the originals.
